# Priority Model

Priority should combine impact and urgency.

## Example Matrix

| Impact \\ Urgency | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | P1 | P1/P2 | P2 |
| Medium | P2 | P3 | P3 |
| Low | P3 | P4 | P4 |

## Examples

### P1 Critical
- Major outage
- Critical security incident
- Business-critical platform unavailable for many users

### P2 High
- Department-wide disruption
- Key process blocked
- No practical workaround

### P3 Medium
- Individual operational issue
- Workaround exists

### P4 Low
- Standard request
- Minor issue
- Information request

Thresholds are illustrative and should be aligned with the service catalog.
