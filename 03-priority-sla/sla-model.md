# SLA Model

| Priority | Target First Response | Target Restore / Resolution |
|---|---:|---:|
| P1 | 15 min | 4 hours |
| P2 | 30 min | 8 business hours |
| P3 | 2 business hours | 2 business days |
| P4 | 4 business hours | 5 business days |

These are fictional example targets.

## SLA Awareness

The agent may:
- identify SLA risk,
- flag overdue tickets,
- recommend escalation.

The agent should not alter SLA targets unless authorized configuration is provided.
